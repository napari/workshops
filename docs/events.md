Live workshops are instructor-led and run in small cohorts. They are delivered
in person at conferences and online. Self-guided versions of every session
are always freely available — see the [workshop catalogue](#home) for the
workshops and their session lengths.

Interested in attending, hosting, or sponsoring a workshop?
Reach out on [Zulip](https://napari.zulipchat.com) or the
[community forum](https://forum.image.sc/tag/napari).
